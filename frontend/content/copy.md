## About

### Description

FORMA is a 3-day summit bringing together architects, urban planners, engineers, and designers to explore sustainable futures, urban transformation, and technology in the built environment.

From net-zero construction to AI-driven design, FORMA gathers the sharpest minds at the intersection of creativity and engineering. Join 1,200+ professionals for keynotes, panels, workshops, and conversations that shape how we build.

### Key Stats

- **1,200+** Attendees
- **40+** Speakers
- **25** Countries Represented

## Speakers

### Elena Vasquez
- **Role**: Founding Partner
- **Company**: Studio Vasquez
- **Bio**: Elena leads one of Europe's most awarded sustainable architecture practices. Her firm's work on carbon-positive buildings has been featured in Dezeen, Wallpaper*, and the Venice Biennale. She lectures on regenerative design at the AA School.
- **Image**: `speakers/elena-vasquez.jpg`

### James Okonkwo
- **Role**: Director of Urban Design
- **Company**: Metropolitan Studio
- **Bio**: James specialises in resilient urban systems for climate-vulnerable cities. His projects span Lagos, London, and Jakarta, focusing on flood mitigation, green infrastructure, and community-led planning. He is a regular contributor to The Architectural Review.
- **Image**: `speakers/james-okonkwo.jpg`

### Lina Petersson
- **Role**: Head of Computational Design
- **Company**: Forma Architects
- **Bio**: Lina bridges architecture and computer science, leading a team that uses generative algorithms and machine learning to optimise building performance. Her TED talk on algorithmic aesthetics has over 2 million views.
- **Image**: `speakers/lina-petersson.jpg`

### Ravi Mehta
- **Role**: Professor of Sustainable Construction
- **Company**: UCL Bartlett School of Architecture
- **Bio**: Ravi is a leading researcher in mass timber and bio-based construction materials. He advises the UK government on sustainable building regulations and has published over 60 peer-reviewed papers on low-carbon construction methods.
- **Image**: `speakers/ravi-mehta.jpg`

### Sophie Laurent
- **Role**: Founder & Director
- **Company**: UrbanLab Paris
- **Bio**: Sophie's practice focuses on affordable housing and participatory design. Her projects in Marseille, Brussels, and Lisbon have won multiple European housing awards. She advocates for design processes that centre the communities who will live in them.
- **Image**: `speakers/sophie-laurent.jpg`

### Marcus Brandt
- **Role**: Innovation Lead
- **Company**: Arup Engineering
- **Bio**: Marcus heads Arup's smart buildings research division, exploring how sensor networks, digital twins, and predictive systems can make buildings more responsive and efficient. Previously at Foster + Partners, he brings 15 years of engineering innovation.
- **Image**: `speakers/marcus-brandt.jpg`
